Frequently asked questions

Where do the leads come from?
Public posts and comments on social media. Every lead includes the link to the post and the person's profile, so you can see what they said.
Are these purchased lists or form fills?
No. Each lead is a real person with a public post or comment behind it.
Will you sell the same lead to other agents?
No. We work with one realtor per market.
What if a lead is not a fit?
If a lead is a renter, has already bought, or is in the wrong area, we replace it.
How often do I get leads?
Monthly. You choose the delivery date, and each report covers public activity from the previous month.
Do you take a share of my commission?
No. You pay a flat monthly fee for a set number of leads. There is no commission share and no ad spend.
Can I tune the leads to my business?
Yes. Buyers, sellers or both, buy or rent, specific towns or ZIP codes, property types, and what to filter out.
Do you contact the leads for me?
No. You reach out. Each lead comes with a tailored opener based on what they posted.
Is there a contract?
No. Packages are billed monthly in advance and you can stop any month.
